[NSG-Visual-Assets_v1.5.zip](https://access.nullsignal.games/Visual%20Assets/NSG-Visual-Assets_v1.5.zip),

`3ae7f3e0567c96e2b34625663ce4013abe0fa64d7684fc3118f9da768216291f`.
The original game glyph designs are credited to Patrick Burk in the
[2018 art gallery](https://nullsignal.games/blog/artgallery/).

The SVGs come from `SVG/Game Symbols` in that pack. Paths and view boxes are
preserved. Presentation changes: a 64-pixel import size, warm white fills/strokes
for the dark UI, removal of XML/DTD declarations, and descriptive filenames.
Null Signal's asset guidelines explicitly permit minor recoloring. No card
illustrations, card faces, or card metadata are included in this directory.
